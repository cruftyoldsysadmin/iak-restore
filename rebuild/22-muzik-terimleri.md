**Parent:** (top level)

**Terms:** 141 with definitions, 104 without — 245 total
**Source:** rebuilt from the old per-letter pages, terms re-paired to definitions

## Why this is one page now

On the old site the terms and definitions were two separate side-by-side text
blocks. They only lined up while every definition fitted on one line. From
`Allegro` onward — whose definition wraps — every definition was displayed against
the wrong term. A single table cannot drift, so this fixes a fault that was live
on the old site for years.

## ⚠ Needs a musician's check

Letters **C, F** had more terms than definitions, so the pairing
below is positional guesswork for those letters. Unmatched terms were placed at
the end. If a definition is actually missing from the *middle* of one of these
letters, the entries after it are shifted. Roughly ten minutes to verify.
